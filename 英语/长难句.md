## 阅读 Text 2

Coming at a time of fresh incentives from the UK government for landowners to grow  
more trees, the trade body says these don’t go far enough and fail to promote the benefits of  planting them to boost timber supplies

incentives from... for landowners 的意思是来自... 的对 landowners 的刺激，刺激干嘛呢？去 grow more trees

It also encompasses significant hesitation on behalf of farmers and other landowners

encompass 是包含的意思，on behalf of 是介词，用来修饰 hesitation 表示来自于的意思，而不是代表的意思

to invest 也是用来修饰 hesitation 的

on behalf of 以及 to invext 都是用来修饰 hesitation 的后置定语

## 新题型

No college will expect that a student has a huge laundry list of extracurriculars that they  have been passionately involved in each for an extended period of time.

后面的 that 是用来修饰  list of extracurriculars  的，什么样的  list of extracurriculars  呢？一个中学生充满激情的参加了 lis 上的每个活动并且花费了一段多余时间的 list


Experts say it is tougher to distinguish oneself in a school-affiliated extracurricular  activity that is common among high school students than it is to stand out while doing an  uncommon activity.

这个句子的主体是 it is ... tougher than (it is)....，这是一个固定搭配，这两个 it 都做形式主语，than 前面的真正的主语是 to distinguish oneself in a school-affiliated extracurricular  activity , 跟了一个 that 从句；than 之后的真正主语是 to stand out while doing an  uncommon activity，另外 than 后面的 it is 经常省略，这里完整写出来了，有点臃肿



A Maryland high school guidance counselor, says  unconventional extracurricular activities can help students impress college admissions offices,  
assuming they demonstrated serious commitment.

这里的 assuming 用作条件从句，其实跟 if 引导的类似，只不过这里 assuming 的语气更重一些，表示只要.....（或者翻译成前提是.....），而 if 是如果......
