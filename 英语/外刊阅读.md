### 长难句&不常见的表达

IT is hard to ignore just how bitterly divided the world feels right now. （bitterly 这里作副词用来修饰 divided，表示分裂的程度非常深）

 Online, debates spiral into hostility within seconds. In person, contentious topics are often avoided altogether. （一种线上和线下对比）

Early hominins were prey as much as predator, constantly threatened by dangers like sabre-toothed tigers or giant hyenas. 

Gray invites us to reframe this instinct, urging us to see adversaries not as predators, but as fellow humans, motivated by the same need to prevent harm.（predators 和 fellow humans 是并列的, motivated 是用来修饰 fellow human 的定语）

Instead, he argues, all morality is rooted in perceptions of harm, and differences arise from what each group happens to deem harmful. (instead 这里翻译成但是，what 引导宾语从句跟在 from 后面)

That notion leads to the heart of Outraged: the concept of moral humility, which translates into the ability to recognise the limits of your own perspective while respecting the validity of the moral convictions of others.

Instead, personal stories of harm are far more effective in fostering empathy and understanding.（表示转折，相对于上文的客观数据，个人伤害的故事更能促进同情心）
