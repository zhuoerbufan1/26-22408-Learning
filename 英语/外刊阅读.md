The decision enthrones the high court—an unelected majority—as a group of technically incompetent, in some cases corrupt, politicos in robes with power over matters that hinge on vital facts about pollution, medicine, employment, and much else. These matters govern our lives.==(of technically ... 用来修饰 group 的，in some cases 修饰 group, politicos in robes 也是用来修饰 group， with power.... 用来修饰 politicos；这段话的大致意思是上文所说的决定作为一个组织登顶了最高法院，剩下的三段话都是修饰这个组织的)==
