# 功能句

## 小作文

### 寒暄 （3 句）

（1）I am glad to hear that....
（2）I am deeply sorry to hear that ....
（3）I am quite pleased to recall ...（感谢信中的寒暄）

### 写信目的（2句）

（1）the notice is for the purpose of....
（2）I am writing for the purpose of making an apology (making a recommendation ) since....


### 建议（3 句）

（1）it is adviseable for you to ... since it is only by... that you can....
（2）we are supposed to take immediatel actions to....
（3）it is exceeding necessary for you to.....
### 个人介绍&个人举例（2 句）

（1）As a senior student who is preparing for the national entrance exam, I am quited intrested in.....
（2）A case in point is myself, as a  senior student who is preparing for the national entrance exam, I...


### 介绍意义（1 个排比句）

To conclude, this party can **bring you** oppotunity to make new friends, **arouse your passion for** college life, **and help you** know China better.


### 介绍某个东西内容（1 句）

it is characterized by ....., such as A, B and C（This party is characterized by diverse interesting activities such as singing ,dancing, ）

### 表达感激（1 句）

To begin with, it is your tremendous help that enabled me to enjoy a ....。Thus I am deeply grateful to you for your help.
