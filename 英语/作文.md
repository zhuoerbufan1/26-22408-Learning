# 大作文 - 数据图表

## 图表单词

table：表格
pie chart：扇形图
column chart：柱状图
line chart：折线图

## 描述图表数据的句子

### 上升（2 句）

（1）During this period, the.... ascended sharply, from .... to .....
（2）The chart clearly displays the remarkable increase in the porpotion of ..... from.... to.... in 2023

### 下降（1 句）

（1）The percentage of ... showed an opposite trend, decreasing form ... to... 
（2）上面的上升都可以改成下降

### 稳定 (1 句)

（1）meanwhile, the percentage of.... maintained stable at 12 %

### 波动（1 句）

（1）the number of... **fluctuated** around ....



### 静态图表的一段套路句

Overall, learning ranks first, accounting for 35%, followed by entertainment at 32%. The next are part-jobs and club activities at 14% and 13% respectively. Finally comes exercise,  at 8%.

## 人工智能高科技


### 主题句（用在第二段开头一句联系主旨）

Such a scene and statistics, apparently can be associated with the rapid development of digital technology, which has fundamentally change the way we live, work, and communicate.

### 原因句（正反两句）

（1）正面：For one thing, in recently years, China has been characterized by the economic boom and prosperity, which has enabled a number of companies to invest numerous money to the development of artificial intelligence, which has been widely adopted in fields such as finance and education. 

（2）反面：For another, excessive reliance on AI tools may reduce people's critical thinking and problem-solving abilities, especially among young students. 

### 措施

Educational institutions can train students in critical thinking and ai literacy, so that they will not blindly rely on AI tools.
