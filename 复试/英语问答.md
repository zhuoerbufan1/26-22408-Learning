
### Why did you give up your job to take the graduate entrance examination?

I wasn't particularly fond of my previous job, I am a person who really enjoys learning new things. But my previous work was mostly management-oriented and involved a lot of trivial tasks. It didn't make use of the knowledge I acquired during my undergraduate studies. So I want to deepen my understanding and application of knowledge through graduate studies.

### Do you have a study plan if you are enrolled in this major？

 If I am lucky enough to be admitted to this university, I will make good use of the three years at this university. First of all, I will try my best to lay a solid foundation for my further research. Besides, I will be familiar with the latest development in this field by reading related books. In addition, I will actively cooperate with my tutor to complete some research projects. That is  my plan for my postgraduate years.
